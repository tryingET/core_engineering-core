# Product Posture: engineering-core

## Purpose

This document is the product-maturity bridge between durable vision and live execution.

It records what is real now, what semver `v1.0` is intended to mean, the principal proof gaps, and the evidence required to close them. It is not a roadmap, release schedule, task queue, changelog, or authority-bearing decision record.

## Posture in one sentence

Engineering-core v0.10.0 already ships mechanisms across all four intended v1 pillars; v1.0 is earned when those mechanisms operate as one dependable, federated, evidence-calibrated, and review-governed product under representative AI Society owner-group proof.

## Semver commitment

The target posture binds directly to semantic version `v1.0`.

The four pillars below are cumulative properties of one product, not maturity releases named M1–M4 and not a v1→v4 delivery sequence:

1. **Dependable Adoption**
2. **Federated Interoperation**
3. **Evidence Calibration**
4. **Review-Governed Evolution**

All four require a credible baseline before v1.0. They do not need their terminal, ecosystem-scale form before v1.0. Compatible strengthening can continue through v1.x.

The proposed v1 boundary is a deliberate clean break from pre-v1 package releases: v1.0 does not promise to preserve v0 commands, schemas, content behavior, or executable interoperability. From v1.0 forward, public stable surfaces retain compatible meaning through v1.x; package semver does not reset an existing versioned protocol identifier.

The final v1.0 compatibility/rendering manifest is materialized only after bounded pilot dispositions finish, then becomes the assertion-backed baseline for future v1.x comparison. Exact participants, empirical thresholds, and platform budgets remain prospectively accepted qualification facts; they may change without reopening the constitution only when its owner-independence, public contract, authority, and pillar invariants remain unchanged.

No v2, v3, or v4 product contract is defined here. A future major version should require a genuine compatibility break or a materially different product constitution, not an arbitrary strategy horizon.

## Current observed baseline: v0.10.0

Evidence anchors: [v0.10.0 release record](../releases/2026-08-22-v0.10.0-local-release.md), [support policy](../support-policy.md), [product overview](../../README.md), and [authority map](../authority-map.md).

### Product and distribution

- v0.10.0 is a published, pre-1.0 GitHub Release with wheel, source archive, and checksums.
- The product is a versioned CLI plus engineering content, distributed from source and GitHub Releases rather than a package registry or hosted service.
- Linux is the supported operating-system family; Ubuntu Python 3.10–3.13 and rolling Arch are release-validation environments. macOS and Windows remain best effort.
- Latest stable and current `main` are supported for fixes; older pre-1.0 releases are historical evidence and optional transition inputs, not compatibility promises for v1.

### Shipped product surfaces

- Versioned lanes, addenda, disciplines, templates, profiles, stable catalog metadata, and a separate pilot overlay.
- Deterministic retrieval, recommendation, planning, explanation, and static diagnostics.
- Dry-run-first initialization and migration with structural adoption scans, baselines, and warning-first ratchets.
- Explicit-population capability observation without consumer command execution.
- Provider-neutral advice validation, owner receipts and dispositions, work packets, and evidence reconciliation with explicit authority ceilings.
- Reproducible release-lineage, self-check, package, and platform validation contracts.

### Current authority posture

- Repository owners retain command, validation, deviation, exception, rollout, and landing authority.
- Agent Kernel retains live direction, task, decision, evidence, and lineage authority.
- Scope owners retain repository populations, generated dashboards, remediation, and adoption-wave authority.
- DSPx/Oracle or another explicitly selected empirical owner must own causal or effectiveness analysis.
- Ontology-kernel and ROCS retain canonical shared-semantic ownership and validation boundaries.
- Prompt Vault retains reusable prompt and procedure authority.
- The current AK direction state contains one active strategic frame for trustworthy, adoptable, evidence-bounded engineering guidance. No product-posture statement creates a task or implementation wave.

## Cumulative v1 release rule

A v1.0 claim requires all four pillars at their bounded baseline. Strength in one pillar cannot compensate for failure in another:

- easy adoption cannot excuse centralized authority;
- federation cannot excuse ambiguous or unsafe contracts;
- evidence cannot promote itself into doctrine or governance truth;
- legitimate review cannot make a false effectiveness claim true;
- learning cannot weaken deterministic behavior, privacy, v1 compatibility, reversibility, or owner exit.

The representative population, success thresholds, review owners, evidence forms, counterevidence policy, and rollback expectations must be declared before a v1 release candidate is judged. This document intentionally does not invent those thresholds.

## Initial v1 proof scope

The initial v1 proof population is independently governed **AI Society owner groups**.

External organizations are not required to declare v1.0. Public portability, immutable installation, explicit authority boundaries, and absence of hidden workspace knowledge remain product requirements so external adoption is possible without redesign.

Evidence from one repository, one company, one maintainer, or one model is insufficient for cross-owner maturity claims.

## Target v1 operator experience

1. A maintainer selects an immutable release and receives only applicable guidance.
2. The product explains selections, dependencies, omissions, pilots, and local deviations before any change.
3. Clean adoption or an explicitly supported transition is dry-run-first, inspectable, idempotent where promised, recoverable, and reversible.
4. V1 compatibility, unsupported history, observed execution, supplied evidence, empirical analysis, and authority-bearing decisions remain visibly distinct.
5. Scope owners can combine bounded observations without surrendering population or rollout control.
6. Evidence can improve recommendations while preserving abstention, counterevidence, and falsification.
7. Shared guidance changes only through review-governed lifecycle decisions with compatibility and rollback paths.
8. A consumer can decline or remove engineering-core without losing repository runtime truth or authority.

## Principal current strengths

- Broad deterministic primitives already exist; v1 does not require inventing four new products.
- Authority ceilings are explicit across scanner, advisor, evidence, and owner-use contracts.
- Safe-input, provenance, release-lineage, and compatibility mechanisms provide a strong assurance base.
- Repo-local policy and immutable release pins make adoption inspectable and reversible.
- Stable and pilot catalog surfaces are separated, supporting evidence-gated evolution.

## Principal current gaps

- The breadth of shipped mechanisms has stronger implementation proof than whole-product usability proof.
- Cross-owner federation has not yet demonstrated routine interoperability and truthful failure under realistic skew and incomplete data.
- Evidence records remain bounded claims; representative outcome improvement and calibration are not established.
- Review-governed doctrine evolution is specified more strongly than it is operationally demonstrated across independent owners.
- v1 release thresholds, exact participating owner groups and repositories, review assignments, and evidence protocols still require predeclaration through the owning Agent Kernel decision surface before release candidacy.

## Convergence priorities

The highest-leverage convergence is not indiscriminate feature growth. It is:

1. freeze the coherent v1 public contract and define predeclared release evidence;
2. exercise complete clean-adoption, supported-transition, recovery, rollback, and removal journeys with independent AI Society owners;
3. prove cross-owner interoperability under skew, partial data, privacy limits, and malicious inputs;
4. run bounded prospective calibration studies through the proper empirical owner surface;
5. complete non-coercive review-governed doctrine cycles plus deterministic transition and rollback conformance;
6. declare v1.0 only when the cumulative gate is satisfied.

These are product-level convergence priorities. Concrete sequencing, tasks, decisions, and evidence belong in Agent Kernel and the relevant owner repositories.

## Hard status-language rules

- Describe mechanisms as shipped only when source, tests, release artifacts, or published documentation establish that fact.
- Describe maturity as demonstrated only when the declared evidence gate has passed.
- Never equate structural adoption with effectiveness, a matched receipt with authenticated execution, or empirical superiority with governance authority.
- Never call a target state current merely because its component schemas or commands exist.
- Preserve negative results, omissions, unknowns, abstentions, and counterevidence.
